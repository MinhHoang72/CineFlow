3.2  Entity  Descriptions  [Provide  one  block  per  entity  with  descriptions  and  key  business  attributes  only.]  
Entity:  User  
Field  Value  
Purpose  
Represents  every  person  with  an  account  in  the  CineFlow  system,  including  Customer,  Staff,  Manager,  and  Admin.   Key  business  attributes  
Full  name,  account,  email  address,  role,  assigned  branch,  account  status   
Business  identity  
Accounts  and  email  addresses  are  unique  across  the  system.  Staff  accounts  can  be  assigned  to  a  specific  cinema  branch.   
Status  /  lifecycle  Active  ·  Inactive  ·  Locked   
\n=== PAGE 11 ===\nEntity:  Movie   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  movie  managed  and  displayed  in  the  CineFlow  system  for  customer  discovery  and  cinema  screening.   Key  business  attributes  
Title,  synopsis,  duration,  poster  image,  genre,  age  rating,  release  status   
Business  identity  Each  movie  is  uniquely  identified  by  MovieID.   
Status  /  lifecycle  Coming  Soon  ·  Now  Showing  ·  Ended  ·  Archived   
Entities:  Branch   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  physical  cinema  branch  where  movies  are  screened  and  customers  can  make  booking   Key  business  attributes  
Branch  name,  address,  city,  contact  information,  operational  status   
Business  identity  Branch  is  uniquely  identified  by  BranchID.   Status  /  lifecycle  Active  ·  Inactive  ·  Temporarily  closed   
Entities:  Hall 
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  screening  hall  within  a  cinema  branch  where  movie  showtimes  are  organized.   Key  business  attributes  
Hall  name,  hall  type(Standard  2D  or  3D),  capacity,  branch   
Business  identity  Hall  is  uniquely  identified  by  HallID  within  the  cinema  system.   Status  /  lifecycle  Available  ·  Maintenance  ·  Inactive   
Entities:  Seat   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  an  individual  seat  available  for  customers  during  a  movie  screening.  Key  business  attributes  
Seat  number,  seat  type  (Standard,  VIP,  Sweetbox),  hall,  seat  maintenance  status   
Business  identity  Seat  is  uniquely  identified  by  SeatID  and  its  seat  number  is  unique  within  a  hall.   Status  /  lifecycle  Available  ·  Reserved  ·  Unavailable   
Entities:  Showtime   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  scheduled  screening  of  a  movie  at  a  specific  cinema  hall  and  time.   Key  business  attributes  
Movie,  branch,  hall,  start  time,  end  time,  base  ticket  price,  showtime  status   
Business  identity  Showtime  is  uniquely  identified  by  ShowtimeID  and  is  associated  with  one  movie  and  one  hall.   Status  /  lifecycle  Scheduled  ·  Ongoing  ·  Completed  ·  Cancelled   
 Entity:  Booking   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  booking  order  created  by  a  customer  or  staff  for  selected  seats  and  services  of  a  specific  movie  showtime.   \n=== PAGE 12 ===\nKey  business  attributes  
Customer,  showtime,  selected  seats,  booking  date,  total  amount,  booking  status   
Business  identity  Booking  is  uniquely  identified  by  BookingID.   Status  /  lifecycle  Pending  Payment  ·  Confirmed  ·  Completed  ·  Cancelled  ·  Expired   
Entities:  Ticket   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  digital  movie  ticket  generated  after  a  successful  booking  and  used  for  cinema  entry.   Key  business  attributes  
Booking,  showtime,  seat,  ticket  code,  QR  code,  ticket  status   
Business  identity  Ticket  is  uniquely  identified  by  TicketID  and  has  a  unique  Ticket  Code  for  verification.   Status  /  lifecycle  Valid  ·  Used  ·  Cancelled  ·  Expired   
Entities:  Payment   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  payment  transaction  associated  with  a  booking  or  F&B  order.   Key  business  attributes  
Booking,  payment  amount,  payment  method,  transaction  code,  payment  status   
Business  identity  Payment  is  uniquely  identified  by  PaymentID  and  transaction  code.   Status  /  lifecycle  Pending  ·  Successful  ·  Failed  ·  Cancelled  ·  Timeout   
Entities:  Promotion   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  promotion  configuration  used  to  support  voucher  and  discount  activities  in  the  cinema  system.   Key  business  attributes  
Promotion  name,  discount  information,  start  date,  end  date,  promotion  status   
Business  identity  Promotion  is  uniquely  identified  by  PromotionID.   Status  /  lifecycle  Scheduled  ·  Active  ·  Expired  ·  Inactive   
Entities:  Voucher   
Field  Value  Purpose  Represents  a  discount  code  that  customers  can  apply  to  eligible  bookings.   Key  business  attributes  
Voucher  code,  promotion,  discount  value,  validity  period,  usage  conditions   
Business  identity  Voucher  code  is  unique  across  the  system.   Status  /  lifecycle  Available  ·  Used  ·  Expired  ·  Disabled    3.3  Data  Business  Rules  [Rules  about  data  that  are  not  obvious  from  the  ERD.  ]  Rule  Description  Enforced  On  Unique  account   No  two  users  may  share  the  same  account  name,  regardless  of  role.   
Account  creation  and  account  change   Unique  email   No  two  users  may  share  the  same  email  address.   Account  creation  and  email  change   Unique  ticket  code   No  two  tickets  may  share  the  same  Ticket  Code.  Each  generated  ticket  must  be  uniquely  identifiable  for  verification.   
Ticket  generation   \n=== PAGE 13 ===\nStaff  must  have  a  centre  
A  Staff  account  must  be  assigned  to  a  specific  cinema  centre  before  performing  branch-level  staff  operations.   
Staff  account  assignment   
Movie  must  be  available  for  distribution   
Only  a  movie  with  an  appropriate  release  status  may  be  assigned  to  a  cinema  branch  for  screening.   
Movie  distribution   
No  overlapping  showtimes   
Two  showtimes  in  the  same  hall  cannot  have  overlapping  screening  periods.   
Showtime  creation  and  update   Showtimes  require  valid  movie  and  hall   
A  showtime  must  reference  an  existing  movie  and  an  existing  cinema  hall.   
Showtime  creation  and  update   Seat  must  be  available  for  booking   
A  customer  may  only  select  seats  that  are  available  for  the  selected  showtime.   
Seat  selection  and  booking   No  double  booking   A  seat  cannot  be  confirmed  in  more  than  one  booking  for  the  same  showtime.   
Booking  confirmation   
Expired  seat  locks  are  released   
Temporarily  locked  seats  must  be  released  when  the  booking  or  payment  time  limit  expires.   
Seat  reservation   
Voucher  must  be  valid   
A  voucher  may  only  be  applied  when  it  is  within  its  validity  period,  enabled,  and  satisfies  its  usage  conditions.   
Voucher  application   
Unique  voucher  code   No  two  vouchers  may  share  the  same  voucher  code.   Voucher  creation  and  voucher  code  change   
Successful  payment  confirms  booking   
A  booking  can  only  be  changed  to  Confirmed  after  the  associated  payment  is  successful.   
Payment  confirmation   
Booking  status  follows  defined  lifecycle   
A  booking  must  use  one  of  the  defined  statuses:  Pending  Payment,  Confirmed,  Completed,  Cancelled,  or  Expired.   
Booking  status  update   
Used  ticket  cannot  be  reused   
A  ticket  marked  as  Used  cannot  be  checked  in  again.   Ticket  check-in   
No  refund  or  exchange   
The  system  does  not  support  refund  or  ticket  exchange  processing.   
Booking  and  payment  processing
